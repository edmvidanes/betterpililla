# Pililla, Rizal public-document collection checklist

Prepared 5 November 2026. These are draft document scopes. No request has been sent.

Use the public electronic version where available. Request public institutional records only. Confirm the custodian and official receiving channel before delivery; no unverified email or telephone number is supplied here.

| Priority / phase | Custodian to verify | Documents to request | Required context |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 / Phase 2 | Municipal administrator / Citizen's Charter coordinator | Latest Citizen's Charter, amendments, current office directory and approved public hotline list | Edition, effective date, service page, requirements, fee, processing time, responsible office, public hours and contact verification date |
| 2 / Phase 3 | Secretary to the Sangguniang Bayan | Ordinance and resolution index plus full texts for 2022–2026; current organizational resolution and committee assignments | Number, series, adoption date, title, approval/effectivity, amendment or repeal references; identify unavailable years |
| 3 / Phase 3 | DILG / municipal administrator | Confirm changes to the February 2026 elective-official directory; current ex-officio seats and replacements | Term, effective dates and supporting proclamation, appointment or succession record |
| 4 / Phase 4 | PSA / municipal planning office | Latest Pililla PSGC and barangay roster; 2020 and 2024 population tables; published geographic profile | Reference date, units, geographic scope, methodology and source table; no estimated missing values |
| 5 / Phase 4 | DTI / BLGF / municipal treasurer | Pililla CMCI profile by year; DOF 074-2024 annex row and applicable later orders; annual income records | Ranking cohort, indicators, income classification effective date, fiscal year, income definition and units |
| 6 / Phase 5 | Budget officer / accountant / treasurer / BAC / COA | 2023–2025 budgets, financial statements, SRE, AIP, APP, audit reports and responses; available 2026 quarterly reports | Fiscal year, fund, document version, publication URL, completeness and coverage; distinguish appropriation from actual expenditure |
| 7 / Phase 6 | Tourism office / DRRMO / municipal health office / archives | Official place listings and current visitor details; public advisories; primary town-history references | Issued and expiry dates for advisories; current visitor access, fees and hours; archival identifiers and citations |

For every response, retain the source file and capture date, compute its SHA-256, check that it identifies Pililla in Rizal, and record page/table/row locators. Publish only after content review. If no document exists or it is unavailable, record that response and keep the section WorkInProgress.

Suggested request text: “Please provide the publicly releasable electronic copies of [documents and periods above] for the Municipality of Pililla, Rizal. Include their edition/reference dates and official publication links. If a requested record is unavailable, please identify the missing period and the appropriate custodian. We will cite the original records and keep unavailable fields blank.”
