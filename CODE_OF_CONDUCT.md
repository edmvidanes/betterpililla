# Code of Conduct

BetterPililla welcomes contributors of different backgrounds, experience levels and views. Treat everyone with respect and focus discussion on evidence and the work.

We expect considerate language, useful feedback, respect for privacy and willingness to correct mistakes. Disagreement is welcome when it addresses ideas rather than people.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, spam and knowingly misleading public records are unacceptable. Do not post private personal information or target public-service staff or residents.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close contributions or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community. Responses should be proportionate to the behavior and its impact.

Use GitHub's report-abuse tools for private safety reports; do not publish sensitive details in issues. For an ordinary moderation concern, mention the maintainer in the relevant discussion without including private information.

This applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions and other spaces managed by BetterPililla. GitHub is not an emergency or official government complaint channel.
